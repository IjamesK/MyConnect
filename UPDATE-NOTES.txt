Behavior

- A Wi-Fi flow records answers and supports uncertainty, alternative checks, recovery and support handoff without depending on the LLM.
- Completed checks and recent symptoms are carried into the support form for review. Nothing submits automatically.
- New conversation clears diagnostic state and ignores an older model response still in flight.
- Empty light selection is not diagnosed as no power unless the user explicitly confirms all lights are off.
- The model instructions also require alternative checks after uncertainty.
